# Stellaris 4.4.6 "Pegasus" Mechanics Overlay

<!-- Advisor-relevant stable 4.4.6 automation corrections. -->

### Automation Reliability
* **Astral Rift Automation:** Automated Science Ships return normally after exploring Astral Rifts; do not warn that automated Rift exploration permanently loses the ship or strands its Scientist.
* **Hostile Avoidance:** Automated ships retain automation and their current construction, research, exploration, or special-order task when avoiding hostile fleets. Temporary evasion does not require the player to rebuild the automation queue.
